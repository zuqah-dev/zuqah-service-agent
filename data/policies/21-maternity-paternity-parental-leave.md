# Maternity, Paternity and Parental Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

Zuqah Technologies recognises that becoming a parent is a significant life
event. This policy describes the leave and support available to employees
who are expecting, adopting or welcoming a new child, and sets out the
company's commitments during and after parental leave.

## 2. Maternity leave

### 2.1 Entitlement

Maternity leave is provided in accordance with the **Maternity Benefit Act,
1961 (as amended in 2017)**.

| Circumstance | Paid leave entitlement |
| --- | --- |
| First or second child | **26 weeks** |
| Third child or more | **12 weeks** |
| Adoption of a child below 3 months of age | **12 weeks** from the date of adoption |
| Commissioning mother (surrogacy) | **12 weeks** from birth of child |

Leave may begin up to 8 weeks before the expected date of delivery. The
remaining leave is available after delivery.

### 2.2 Pay during maternity leave

Maternity leave is fully paid at the employee's regular salary for the
applicable period. This pay is provided by Zuqah Technologies and is separate
from any statutory benefits the employee may be entitled to.

### 2.3 Miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy

An employee who suffers a miscarriage or undergoes a medical termination of
pregnancy is entitled to **6 weeks of paid leave** from the date of the event,
supported by a medical certificate.

### 2.4 Illness arising from pregnancy

If an employee suffers an illness arising from pregnancy or delivery, additional
paid leave of up to **1 month** may be granted on production of a medical
certificate.

### 2.5 Nursing breaks

After returning from maternity leave, an employee is entitled to two breaks
of **15 minutes each** per working day to nurse her child, until the child is
**15 months old**. These breaks are in addition to the regular lunch break
and are paid.

### 2.6 Crèche

Zuqah Technologies will either provide or arrange access to a crèche facility,
as required under the Maternity Benefit Act for employers with 50 or more
employees. Where the company does not yet meet this threshold, it will provide
reasonable support to help employees with childcare arrangements.

### 2.7 Work from home after maternity leave

Where the nature of the role permits, an employee returning from maternity
leave may request a temporary or permanent work-from-home arrangement. Such
requests will be considered under the Flexible Working Arrangements Policy.

### 2.8 Protection from dismissal

An employee may not be dismissed, discharged or have their service terminated
during the period of maternity leave, or in connection with her pregnancy.
Any order of dismissal during this period is void.

## 3. Paternity leave

### 3.1 Entitlement

Zuqah Technologies provides **10 working days of paid paternity leave** to
employees whose partner has given birth or adopted a child below the age of
5 years.

This leave is in addition to the employee's regular annual leave.

### 3.2 When to take it

Paternity leave must be taken within **3 months** of the birth or adoption.
It can be taken in one block or split into two periods, by agreement with
the manager.

### 3.3 Pay during paternity leave

Paternity leave is fully paid at the employee's regular salary.

## 4. Adoption leave

### 4.1 Primary adopter

An employee who is the primary adopter of a child below the age of 3 months
is entitled to **12 weeks of paid leave**, beginning from the date of adoption.
(Note: employees who adopt a child aged 3 months or older are entitled to
paternity leave as applicable.)

### 4.2 Secondary adopter

The employee who is the secondary adopter in an adoption is entitled to
**10 working days of paid adoption leave**, to be taken within 3 months
of the adoption.

## 5. Shared parental leave

Parents may discuss with People Operations whether responsibilities during
the statutory maternity or adoption period can be shared between them. People
Operations will advise on the arrangements available under applicable law
at the time of the request.

## 6. How to apply

### 6.1 Notice requirements

| Leave type | Notice required |
| --- | --- |
| Maternity leave | At least **8 weeks** before the expected date of leave |
| Paternity leave | At least **2 weeks** before the expected leave date |
| Adoption leave | As soon as reasonably possible after adoption is confirmed |

Notice should be given in writing to the employee's manager, with a copy to
People Operations.

### 6.2 Documentation required

| Leave type | Documentation |
| --- | --- |
| Maternity leave | Medical certificate stating expected date of delivery |
| Miscarriage / termination leave | Medical certificate |
| Adoption leave | Proof of adoption (court order or adoption agency confirmation) |

### 6.3 Keeping in touch

During maternity or adoption leave, employees may, by mutual agreement with
their manager, work up to **10 Keeping in Touch (KIT) days** without losing
their parental leave entitlement. KIT days are paid at the employee's normal
daily rate and are used for activities such as training, project meetings or
handover.

KIT days are voluntary and must not be required by the manager.

## 7. Return to work

### 7.1 Right to return

An employee returning from parental leave has the right to return to the
same role on the same terms and conditions. If the role no longer exists due
to a restructuring, the employee must be offered a suitable alternative role
on terms no less favourable.

### 7.2 Phased return

Employees returning from maternity or adoption leave of 12 weeks or more
may request a phased return to full-time hours over a period of up to
**4 weeks**. The phased return is paid at full salary.

### 7.3 Flexible working request

Any employee returning from maternity, paternity or adoption leave may make
a request for flexible working under the Flexible Working Arrangements Policy.
Such requests made on return from parental leave will be considered with
particular care.

## 8. Benefits during leave

During paid parental leave, all benefits continue as normal, including health
insurance, PF contributions and any other benefits the employee is enrolled in.

Annual leave continues to accrue during the full period of maternity or
adoption leave.

## 9. Period leave (menstrual leave)

In recognition that menstruation can cause significant discomfort, Zuqah
Technologies provides **2 days of paid period leave per month** to employees
who experience menstrual symptoms. This is in addition to regular sick leave
and annual leave entitlements.

Period leave does not require a medical certificate and does not need to be
explained in detail. Employees should mark the leave in the HR system as
"period leave" and inform their manager they will be absent.

Period leave is provided in addition to, not instead of, sick leave.
