# Social Media and External Communication Policy

## 1. Purpose

Social media is a powerful tool for building a professional profile, sharing
knowledge and connecting with the industry. It is also a place where careless
posts can damage Zuqah Technologies' reputation, reveal confidential
information or create legal liability.

This policy sets out what employees may and may not post publicly, and how
to handle press or media enquiries.

## 2. Personal social media use

### 2.1 You own your personal accounts

Employees are free to use social media in their personal time. Zuqah
Technologies does not monitor personal social media activity and does not
expect employees to post about the company.

### 2.2 When your personal posts are covered by this policy

This policy applies when:

- You identify yourself as a Zuqah Technologies employee in your profile
  or in the post itself
- The content is related to Zuqah Technologies, its clients, its employees
  or its work
- The post could reasonably be traced back to your employment at Zuqah
  Technologies even without an explicit mention

If none of the above apply, this policy does not govern your personal posts.

### 2.3 What you should not post

Even when posting in a personal capacity, do not post:

- Confidential information about Zuqah Technologies, including client names,
  commercial terms, financial results, product roadmaps or internal disputes
- Information about specific colleagues, clients or partners that they have
  not publicly shared themselves
- Defamatory, discriminatory, harassing or threatening statements about any
  person or group
- Statements that could mislead the public about Zuqah Technologies' products,
  services or business activities
- Content that violates the Code of Conduct

You may post your professional opinions, share industry articles, write about
your work in general terms and advocate for causes you believe in — including
criticising industry practices. The test is whether the content is harmful to
Zuqah Technologies, its people or its partners.

### 2.4 Add a disclaimer

When sharing professional opinions on topics related to your field, it is
good practice to add: *"Views are my own."*

## 3. LinkedIn specifically

LinkedIn is a professional network and sharing Zuqah Technologies-related
content is encouraged where it builds the company's presence. Employees may:

- Share Zuqah Technologies blog posts, product announcements and job openings
- Write about projects they have worked on in general terms, without sharing
  confidential details
- Endorse and recommend colleagues
- Comment on and share Zuqah Technologies' company page posts

Do not share internal-only communications on LinkedIn, even in edited form.

## 4. Representing Zuqah Technologies on social media

Only the following people are authorised to post on Zuqah Technologies'
official social media accounts:

- The CEO
- The Head of Marketing (or designee)

Employees who identify inaccurate or harmful content about Zuqah Technologies
on social media should flag it to marketing@zuqahtechnologies.com rather than
responding publicly.

## 5. Press, media and analyst enquiries

All enquiries from journalists, analysts, podcast hosts, conference organisers
or any other external media must be routed to:

**marketing@zuqahtechnologies.com**

Do not respond to press enquiries directly, even if you are confident the
information is correct. Background conversations and off-the-record comments
can create risk even if not intended for publication.

If a journalist contacts you directly, you may say: *"Thank you for reaching
out. Please contact our communications team at marketing@zuqahtechnologies.com
and they'll be happy to help."*

## 6. Speaking at conferences and external events

Employees who wish to speak at a conference, podcast, webinar or external
event in a professional capacity and intend to mention Zuqah Technologies or
their role:

1. Inform your manager and People Operations of the invitation
2. Share a draft of the presentation or talking points for review if the
   content relates to Zuqah Technologies work
3. Receive written confirmation that the content is approved

Approval ensures that nothing confidential or legally sensitive is shared and
that the company is represented accurately.

## 7. Customer references and testimonials

Sharing positive experiences with clients or customers publicly (e.g., in a
case study, a LinkedIn post or a press release) requires the client's explicit
written consent. Route all customer reference requests through
marketing@zuqahtechnologies.com.

## 8. Consequences

Violation of this policy — particularly disclosure of confidential information
or conduct that damages Zuqah Technologies' reputation — may result in
disciplinary action up to and including dismissal. In serious cases, legal
action may also be pursued.
