# Expense and Travel Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose and principles

Zuqah Technologies reimburses reasonable costs necessarily incurred in doing your job. Three
principles govern every decision in this policy:

- **Spend it as if it were your own.** Where this policy is silent, that is the test.
- **Book early.** Cost is largely determined at the point of booking.
- **Client agreements take precedence.** Where an engagement specifies different
  limits, those apply and the difference is billed accordingly.

## 2. Limits

All figures are per person and exclusive of tax unless stated. Amounts in other
currencies are the local equivalent, converted at the rate on the transaction date.

| Category | Limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breakfast | $20 | Only when travel starts before 07:00 |
| Lunch | $25 | Only when away from home and office |
| Dinner | $45 | |

### 2.1 High-cost cities

New York, San Francisco, Boston, Chicago, Seattle, London, Zurich, Tokyo and
Singapore attract the higher accommodation limit. The list is reviewed each April.

### 2.2 Exceeding a limit

Occasionally exceeding a limit is acceptable where the circumstances justify it —
a conference hotel with no cheaper option nearby, for instance. Note the reason on
the claim. A claim over a limit with no explanation is returned rather than
declined, and returned claims delay payment by a cycle.

## 3. Travel booking

### 3.1 Air travel

Book through the Zuqah Technologies travel portal. Economy is the standard for all flights
under **6 hours** of scheduled flying time.

Premium economy may be booked for flights over 6 hours. Business class requires
approval from a practice lead and is normally reserved for flights over 9 hours
where the traveller works the following day.

Flights should be booked at least **14 days** in advance where the dates are
known. Bookings inside 7 days require a note explaining why.

### 3.2 Rail

Standard class for journeys under 3 hours; first class is permitted above that
where it is cheaper than the flexible standard fare, which it frequently is.

### 3.3 Accommodation

Book through the travel portal, which holds negotiated rates. Booking outside the
portal is permitted where it is demonstrably cheaper — note the comparison on the
claim.

### 3.4 Car hire

Compact or intermediate class. Decline the rental company's insurance; Zuqah Technologies's
corporate policy covers it. Refuel before returning, as prepaid fuel is
consistently more expensive.

## 4. Making a claim

1. Submit in the expense portal within **30 days** of the expense being incurred.
2. Attach a receipt for anything over **$25**. A card statement is not a receipt.
3. Select the client and engagement code where the cost is billable.
4. Your manager approves within 5 business days.

Payment is made in the **next payroll run** following approval. Claims submitted
after 30 days require an explanation and are paid at Finance Operations'
discretion; claims after 90 days are not paid.

## 5. Client entertainment

Entertaining a client requires **prior** approval from a practice lead. The claim
must record who attended, their organisation, and the business purpose.

The following are never reimbursable, regardless of approval: anything that could
reasonably be perceived as inducement, gifts of cash or equivalent, and
entertainment at venues inappropriate to a professional setting. See the Code of
Conduct.

Where a client's own policy restricts what its staff may accept, that policy
governs. Ask before booking.

## 6. What is not reimbursable

- Personal travel attached to a business trip, including the additional
  accommodation and any change in airfare
- Fines, penalties and parking tickets
- Airport lounge access, unless a delay exceeds 3 hours
- Seat selection, priority boarding and speedy boarding fees
- In-flight internet on flights under 4 hours
- Mini-bar
- Personal items — clothing, toiletries, medication — except where baggage has
  been delayed by a carrier for more than 24 hours, up to $150
- Travel insurance purchased separately; Zuqah Technologies provides corporate cover

## 7. Working from a different location

Working from a location other than your usual one for personal reasons is not
business travel and no costs are reimbursable. Employees intending to work from
another country for more than **10 working days** must notify People Operations
in advance, as there may be tax and immigration consequences. See also the Remote
Access and VPN Policy on connecting from outside approved countries.

## 8. Audit

A sample of claims is audited each month. Deliberately submitting a claim that is
not genuine is treated as a disciplinary matter under the Code of Conduct.
