# IT Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose

Zuqah Technologies provides computing equipment, software and network access
to employees to do their jobs. This policy defines what that equipment and
access may and may not be used for, and what monitoring exists.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers:

- Company-issued laptops, desktops and mobile phones
- The company network (office WiFi and wired connections)
- The company VPN
- All company software and services (Microsoft 365, HR portal, internal tools)
- Any personal device enrolled in MDM

## 3. Permitted use

### 3.1 Primary purpose

Company IT resources are provided for work purposes. Employees must use them
primarily to carry out their job responsibilities.

### 3.2 Incidental personal use

Limited personal use is permitted provided it:

- Does not interfere with the employee's work or the work of others
- Does not consume significant bandwidth (e.g., streaming video for hours)
- Does not involve any of the prohibited activities listed in section 4
- Is not excessive in duration

Examples of acceptable personal use: checking personal email briefly, using
a messaging app, accessing personal banking, reading the news.

## 4. Prohibited use

The following uses of company IT resources are prohibited under all
circumstances:

### 4.1 Illegal activity

- Downloading or distributing copyrighted content without authorisation
- Accessing, creating or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
- Hacking, port scanning or unauthorised access to any system
- Fraud, identity theft or financial crime

### 4.2 Inappropriate content

- Accessing or distributing pornographic or sexually explicit material
- Accessing or distributing material that promotes violence, discrimination or
  hatred against any person or group
- Gambling using company devices or network

### 4.3 Unauthorised software and services

- Installing software not approved by IT (see Software Requests and Licensing
  Policy)
- Using personal cloud storage (Google Drive, personal Dropbox) for company
  data
- Circumventing company security controls, including VPN bypass tools or
  proxy services

### 4.4 Misuse that affects others

- Using company email or messaging to harass, bully or threaten anyone
- Mass-mailing, spam or phishing from company systems
- Disrupting network services through excessive bandwidth use

### 4.5 Reputational harm

- Posting statements on social media that could harm Zuqah Technologies'
  reputation (see Social Media and External Communication Policy)
- Sharing confidential company information through personal channels

## 5. Monitoring

### 5.1 What is monitored

Zuqah Technologies monitors its IT systems and network for security, compliance
and operational purposes. This monitoring includes:

| Monitored | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Network traffic metadata (sites visited, data volume) | Security threat detection |
| Login events and access logs | Security monitoring and incident investigation |
| Email metadata (sender, recipient, subject, timestamp) | Compliance and investigation |
| Device health and policy compliance status | Security posture management |
| Application usage on company devices | Software licence management |

### 5.2 What is not routinely monitored

- Content of personal emails accessed via a personal account on company devices
- Personal browsing content (URLs are captured for filtering, not content)
- Personal messages on personal applications

### 5.3 When deeper investigation occurs

In the context of a specific security incident, disciplinary investigation or
legal requirement, IT Security may, with senior management authorisation,
access the content of communications on company systems. Employees should
have no expectation of privacy for communications made on company systems,
including company email.

### 5.4 Notice

This policy constitutes notice that monitoring takes place. No additional
notification will be given before monitoring occurs.

## 6. Security obligations

All employees using company IT must:

- Lock their screen when leaving their device unattended
- Not share login credentials with colleagues, even temporarily
- Report security incidents and suspicious activity promptly
- Follow the Password and MFA Standard
- Not disable or bypass any company-installed security software

Full details in the Cybersecurity Awareness Policy and Password and MFA
Standard.

## 7. Software installation

Employees may not install software on company devices without IT approval.
To request software, raise a request through the IT portal under
**Software → New Request**. See the Software Requests and Licensing Policy.

## 8. Consequences of misuse

Violation of this policy may result in:

- Loss of IT access privileges
- Formal disciplinary action under the Grievance and Disciplinary Procedure
- Dismissal for serious violations
- Referral to law enforcement where illegal activity is involved

## 9. Questions

If you are unsure whether a particular use is permitted, ask IT before
proceeding: it@zuqahtechnologies.com
