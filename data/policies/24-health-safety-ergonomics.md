# Health, Safety and Ergonomics Policy

## 1. Purpose

Zuqah Technologies is committed to providing a safe, healthy and ergonomic
working environment for all employees, whether working from the office or from
home. This policy sets out the company's responsibilities and those of
employees.

## 2. Office safety

### 2.1 General obligations

Zuqah Technologies will:

- Maintain office premises in a safe condition, including emergency exits,
  fire suppression equipment and first aid kits
- Conduct a formal safety inspection of office premises at least once per year
- Ensure emergency procedures are posted visibly throughout the office
- Appoint at least one trained First Aider per floor or per 25 employees,
  whichever results in more First Aiders
- Conduct fire drills at least twice per year

Employees must:

- Familiarise themselves with emergency exits and assembly points on joining
- Not obstruct emergency exits or fire equipment
- Report unsafe conditions (broken equipment, spills, electrical hazards)
  to the Facilities team at facilities@zuqahtechnologies.com

### 2.2 Fire emergency

In the event of a fire alarm:

1. Stop work and exit the building immediately via the nearest emergency exit
2. Do not use lifts
3. Proceed to the designated assembly point (marked on each floor's emergency
   notice)
4. Do not re-enter the building until authorised by the fire service or the
   Facilities team

If you discover a fire and it is safe to do so, raise the alarm before
evacuating.

### 2.3 First aid

First Aid kits are located at reception and at the first aid station on each
floor. The name and contact number of the First Aider on each floor is posted
on the notice board and in the HR portal.

For a medical emergency, call emergency services (112 in India) and then
notify the First Aider and the Facilities team.

## 3. Home office ergonomics

### 3.1 Setting up your workspace

Poor ergonomics cause musculoskeletal problems over time. Every employee
working from home should set up their workspace with the following in mind:

| Element | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Chair** | Adjustable height; feet flat on the floor; back supported |
| **Screen height** | Top of screen at or just below eye level; 50–70 cm from eyes |
| **Keyboard and mouse** | Elbows at approximately 90° when typing; wrists neutral |
| **Lighting** | Natural light where possible; screen perpendicular to windows to avoid glare |
| **Breaks** | Take a 5-minute break every 45–60 minutes; stand, stretch, walk |

### 3.2 Equipment for home working

Employees who work from home regularly (3 or more days per week) may request
the following equipment, funded by Zuqah Technologies:

| Item | Maximum provision |
| --- | --- |
| External monitor | One monitor up to ₹15,000 |
| Keyboard and mouse | One set up to ₹3,000 |
| Laptop stand | One stand up to ₹2,000 |
| Ergonomic chair | Up to ₹8,000 contribution towards cost |
| Headset | One headset up to ₹3,000 |

To request equipment, submit a request through the HR portal under
**Equipment → Home Office Request**. Manager approval is required. Equipment
funded by Zuqah Technologies remains company property and must be returned
on departure.

### 3.3 Home office assessment

New employees are encouraged to complete a self-assessment of their home
workstation using the checklist in the HR portal (**Safety → Home Assessment**)
within their first 30 days. Employees with a disability or a pre-existing
musculoskeletal condition may request a professional DSE (Display Screen
Equipment) assessment.

## 4. Accident and incident reporting

### 4.1 What must be reported

Any of the following must be reported, regardless of whether an injury occurred:

- An accident or near-miss at the office or on company business
- An injury sustained while working, including at a home office
- A condition that the employee believes was caused or worsened by work

### 4.2 How to report

1. For urgent medical attention, call 112 and then alert a First Aider or manager
2. Complete an incident report in the HR portal under **Safety → Report Incident**
   within **24 hours** of the event
3. The manager will be notified automatically and will follow up within 1 working day

Reports are kept confidential and are used to improve safety conditions.
Reporting an incident will not result in disciplinary action against the
employee.

### 4.3 Investigation

Zuqah Technologies will investigate all reported incidents to identify root
causes and prevent recurrence. The employee who reported the incident will
be informed of the outcome.

## 5. Occupational health

Where an employee has a health condition that affects their ability to work,
Zuqah Technologies may refer the employee (with their consent) to an
occupational health adviser. The adviser will recommend adjustments to support
the employee in their role.

Reasonable adjustments will be made in accordance with the Anti-Discrimination
and Equal Opportunity Policy.

## 6. Employee responsibilities

All employees must:

- Take reasonable care for their own health and safety and that of colleagues
- Follow all safety procedures and instructions
- Report unsafe conditions, accidents and near-misses promptly
- Not interfere with or misuse safety equipment
- Attend any mandatory safety training
- Inform their manager if a health condition affects their ability to work safely

Failure to follow safety procedures may result in disciplinary action.

## 7. Stress and mental wellbeing

Work-related stress is a health and safety issue. Managers should be alert to
signs of stress in their teams and act early. Employees experiencing stress
should speak to their manager or People Operations. See the Mental Health and
Wellbeing Policy for the support available.

## 8. Pregnancy and nursing

Pregnant employees and those who have recently given birth or are nursing will
be offered a risk assessment of their workspace and duties. Adjustments
(seating, reduced standing time, lighter physical tasks) will be made where
identified as necessary. See also the Maternity, Paternity and Parental Leave
Policy.
