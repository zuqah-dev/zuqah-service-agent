# Laptop Refresh and Hardware Standards

## 1. Purpose

This document sets out which devices Zuqah Technologies issues, when they are replaced, and
what happens when hardware is damaged, lost or fails. It applies to all employees
and to contractors issued Zuqah Technologies equipment.

## 2. Standard device catalogue

Three models are offered. All are configured identically in terms of security and
management, and all are covered by the same support arrangements.

| Model | Specification | Suited to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Zuqah Technologies Book 14** | 14", 16 GB memory, 512 GB storage, 1.2 kg | General consulting, travel-heavy roles |
| **Zuqah Technologies Book 16** | 16", 32 GB memory, 1 TB storage, 1.9 kg | Development, data work, virtualisation |
| **Zuqah Technologies Studio** | 16", 64 GB memory, 2 TB storage, discrete graphics | Machine learning, large-scale modelling |

The Zuqah Technologies Book 14 is the default. Choosing a Zuqah Technologies Book 16 requires no
approval. The Zuqah Technologies Studio requires practice lead approval, recorded on the
request, because of its cost and its materially shorter battery life.

## 3. Refresh cycle

Laptops are replaced on a **three-year** cycle measured from the date of issue,
not from the date of manufacture or purchase.

Eligibility is calculated automatically. Employees receive a notification in the
IT portal **eight weeks** before their device becomes eligible, at which point the
replacement can be ordered. There is no need to raise a ticket to ask whether you
are eligible.

Devices are not replaced early on request. A device that is failing before the
three-year point is repaired or replaced under section 5 as a fault, which is a
different process and does not reset the refresh clock unless the entire unit is
replaced.

## 4. Ordering and delivery

Once eligible:

1. Open the IT portal and select **Request replacement laptop**.
2. Choose a model from section 2.
3. Confirm the delivery address. Home delivery is available in all countries where
   Zuqah Technologies has a legal entity.

Standard delivery is **five to seven business days**. Devices arrive configured
and enrolled; the first sign-in completes setup.

The outgoing device must be returned within **ten business days** of the
replacement arriving. Return packaging and a prepaid label are included in the
box. Devices not returned within thirty days are reported as lost under section 6.

## 5. Faults, damage and repair

### 5.1 Faults under warranty

All Zuqah Technologies laptops carry a three-year manufacturer warranty covering component
failure under normal use — screen, battery, keyboard, storage, mainboard.

Raise a ticket describing the symptom. Where a hardware fault is confirmed, the
usual outcome is an on-site repair visit within **three business days** in
supported locations, or a replacement device where repair is not practical.

Battery capacity below **70%** of original is treated as a fault and replaced
under warranty. Below 80% but above 70% is considered normal wear.

### 5.2 Accidental damage

Accidental damage — drops, liquid, cracked screens — is not covered by warranty
but is covered by Zuqah Technologies's own insurance for the first two incidents per employee
per rolling three-year period. There is no charge to the employee or their
practice for a covered incident.

A third incident within the same period is reviewed with the employee's manager
before a replacement is issued.

### 5.3 What is not covered

Cosmetic wear, missing keycaps that do not affect function, and damage caused by
unauthorised repair or disassembly are not covered. Devices must not be opened by
anyone other than an approved service provider; doing so voids both the warranty
and the insurance.

## 6. Loss and theft

Report a lost or stolen device **immediately**, and in any case within 24 hours,
by contacting IT Support. Speed matters more than completeness of information —
report first, provide detail afterwards.

On report, the device is remotely locked and wiped, and the account's active
sessions are revoked. Where theft is involved, the employee should also file a
police report; the reference number is required for the insurance claim.

A replacement is issued within two business days of the report. Loss is not
treated as a fault or as accidental damage for the purposes of section 5.2 limits.

## 7. Peripherals and accessories

The following are provided on request through the IT portal without approval:

- Docking station, one per employee
- External monitor, up to two per employee
- Keyboard, mouse, headset
- Laptop bag and travel power adapter

Anything outside this list, and any replacement of an item less than two years
old, requires manager approval. Peripherals are not on a refresh cycle and are
replaced on failure.

## 8. Personal use and return

Zuqah Technologies laptops remain Zuqah Technologies property. Reasonable personal use is permitted
subject to the Code of Conduct.

On leaving Zuqah Technologies, all equipment must be returned by the final working day.
Employees are given the opportunity to remove genuinely personal files before
return, but should not assume any data on the device is private — see the Code of
Conduct for the monitoring position.

## 9. Requests outside this standard

Requests for hardware not in the catalogue — a specific model for a customer
project, additional memory for a specific workload, specialist input devices —
should be raised as a ticket with the business justification. These are assessed
individually by End User Computing and are usually answered within five business
days.
