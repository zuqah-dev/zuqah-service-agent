# Grievance and Disciplinary Procedure

## 1. Purpose

This document describes two related but distinct processes:

- **Grievance procedure** — when an employee has a concern or complaint
  about their work, working conditions, or treatment by a colleague or manager
- **Disciplinary procedure** — when Zuqah Technologies needs to address an
  employee's conduct or performance

Both procedures are designed to be fair, consistent and timely. The goal is
resolution, not punishment.

## 2. Grievance procedure

### 2.1 What is a grievance?

A grievance is a concern, problem or complaint raised by an employee about
something Zuqah Technologies has done or failed to do, or about how they
have been treated. Examples include:

- Treatment by a manager that feels unfair or disrespectful
- Disagreement with a pay, promotion or performance decision
- Concerns about working conditions
- Bullying or harassment (non-sexual harassment; sexual harassment is covered
  by the POSH Policy)
- Discrimination (also see the Anti-Discrimination Policy)

### 2.2 Informal resolution

Before raising a formal grievance, employees are encouraged to resolve the
matter informally by speaking to their manager, or to People Operations if
the manager is involved. Many concerns can be resolved quickly this way.

### 2.3 Formal grievance — Step 1: Written complaint

If informal resolution is not possible or appropriate, the employee should
submit a written grievance to People Operations at
people@zuqahtechnologies.com or by letter. The grievance should describe:

- The nature of the concern
- The people involved
- The dates and circumstances
- What resolution the employee is seeking

**Acknowledgement:** People Operations will acknowledge the grievance within
**3 working days** of receipt.

### 2.4 Formal grievance — Step 2: Investigation meeting

People Operations or a nominated manager will invite the employee to a
meeting to discuss the grievance. The employee may bring a colleague or
trade union representative as a companion.

The meeting will normally take place within **10 working days** of the
grievance being acknowledged. Where an investigation is required before
the meeting, People Operations will advise of the expected timeline.

### 2.5 Formal grievance — Step 3: Decision

The investigating manager will provide a written decision within **5 working
days** of the meeting. The decision will include the findings and any action
to be taken.

### 2.6 Appeal

If the employee is not satisfied with the outcome, they may appeal in writing
within **5 working days** of receiving the decision. The appeal will be heard
by a more senior manager or director who was not involved in the original
investigation. The appeal outcome is final.

### 2.7 Timelines summary

| Step | Timeline |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledge written grievance | 3 working days |
| Investigation meeting | Within 10 working days |
| Written decision | Within 5 working days of meeting |
| Appeal hearing | Within 10 working days of appeal |
| Appeal decision | Within 5 working days of appeal hearing |

## 3. Disciplinary procedure

The disciplinary procedure applies where Zuqah Technologies has concerns about
an employee's conduct or performance.

### 3.1 Scope

This procedure applies to all employees, including those on probation. It
does not apply to contractors. Separate performance improvement plans may
apply to performance concerns outside the formal disciplinary process.

### 3.2 Informal action

For minor conduct or performance issues, a manager may address the matter
informally with a conversation or coaching. This will not be recorded on the
employee's HR file.

### 3.3 Investigation

Before any formal disciplinary action is taken, Zuqah Technologies will carry
out a reasonable investigation. The manager will gather relevant information,
which may include speaking to witnesses and reviewing records.

During investigation of a serious allegation, the employee may be suspended
on full pay while the investigation is completed. Suspension is a neutral act
and does not prejudge the outcome.

### 3.4 Disciplinary hearing — Step 1: Notice

The employee will receive written notice of the disciplinary hearing at least
**3 working days** in advance. The notice will include:

- The specific concerns or allegations
- The evidence gathered
- The potential consequences
- The employee's right to bring a companion

### 3.5 Disciplinary hearing — Step 2: The hearing

The hearing gives the employee a full opportunity to respond to the concerns
raised and to present their version of events. They may call witnesses.

### 3.6 Disciplinary outcome

Following the hearing, the manager will decide on one of the following
outcomes, communicated in writing:

| Outcome | When used |
| --- | --- |
| No further action | Concern not substantiated |
| Verbal warning | Minor first offence |
| Written warning | Repeated or more serious offence; recorded for 6 months |
| Final written warning | Serious or repeated; recorded for 12 months |
| Dismissal with notice | Serious misconduct or continued issues after final warning |
| Summary dismissal | Gross misconduct (immediate, without notice) |

### 3.7 Gross misconduct

The following are examples of gross misconduct for which immediate dismissal
may result:

- Theft or fraud
- Physical violence or threats of violence
- Sexual harassment or serious bullying
- Deliberate damage to company property
- Serious breach of data protection obligations
- Falsification of records, including expense claims
- Being under the influence of alcohol or illegal substances at work
- Bringing the company into serious disrepute

This list is not exhaustive.

### 3.8 Appeal

The employee may appeal any disciplinary decision within **5 working days**
of receiving the written outcome. Appeals are heard by a director or senior
manager not previously involved. The appeal decision is final.

### 3.9 Timelines summary

| Step | Timeline |
| --- | --- |
| Notice of disciplinary hearing | At least 3 working days in advance |
| Written outcome | Within 5 working days of hearing |
| Appeal lodged | Within 5 working days of outcome |
| Appeal hearing | Within 10 working days of appeal |

## 4. Right to a companion

In all formal grievance and disciplinary hearings, the employee has the right
to be accompanied by a colleague currently employed by Zuqah Technologies.
This companion may address the hearing, sum up the employee's case and
confer with the employee during the hearing, but may not answer questions
on the employee's behalf.

## 5. Confidentiality

All grievance and disciplinary proceedings are confidential. Parties to the
process, including witnesses, must not discuss the matter outside the process.
Breach of confidentiality may itself lead to disciplinary action.

## 6. No detriment

Raising a genuine grievance or cooperating in a disciplinary investigation
will not result in any detriment. Managers who treat an employee unfavourably
for exercising their rights under this policy will face disciplinary action.
