# Travel Booking Procedure

## 1. Purpose

The Expense and Travel Policy sets the limits for what Zuqah Technologies will
reimburse. This document explains *how* to book travel — which tools to use,
when approval is needed and how to handle changes and cancellations.

## 2. Booking tools

All business travel must be booked through approved channels:

| Travel type | Booking method |
| --- | --- |
| Flights (domestic) | Company travel portal or approved travel agent |
| Flights (international) | Company travel agent only — contact travel@zuqahtechnologies.com |
| Train | Book directly via IRCTC or the app; expense claim after |
| Hotels | Company travel portal or directly (prefer negotiated properties where available) |
| Local cab / ride-hailing | Uber, Ola or auto for travel within the city; expense claim after |
| Car rental | Approved vendor list in the travel portal |

Do not book travel on personal travel sites (MakeMyTrip personal, Booking.com
for hotels, etc.) without confirming with Finance first — rates and policies
may differ, and some personal bookings are not eligible for reimbursement.

## 3. Pre-approval

### 3.1 Domestic travel

Domestic travel does not require advance approval if:
- The trip is within the employee's normal work responsibilities
- The estimated cost falls within the limits in the Expense and Travel Policy

The employee's manager must be informed of travel plans at least **3 working
days** in advance for trips that take the employee out of the office for more
than one day.

### 3.2 International travel

All international travel requires pre-approval, regardless of cost. Submit
a travel approval request through the HR portal under
**Travel → International Travel Request**. Include:

- Destination and dates
- Business purpose
- Estimated total cost (flights, hotel, per diem)
- Manager and Finance sign-off

Submit at least **10 working days** before the intended travel date.
International travel approval takes up to **5 working days**.

### 3.3 Travel exceeding ₹50,000

Any single trip with an estimated total cost above ₹50,000 requires Finance
Director approval, regardless of whether it is domestic or international.

## 4. Booking flights

### 4.1 Class of travel

| Journey duration | Permitted class |
| --- | --- |
| Under 5 hours | Economy |
| 5 hours or more (direct) | Economy (business class requires MD/CEO approval) |
| International, overnight | Economy (Premium economy with Finance Director approval) |

Loyalty programme points may be used to upgrade, but Zuqah Technologies will
not pay the difference for a higher class than permitted.

### 4.2 Advance booking

Book flights as early as practicable. Flights booked within 3 days of departure
are substantially more expensive and will be queried by Finance. Same-day or
next-day bookings require manager sign-off before purchase.

### 4.3 Preferred airports and routes

Use the most direct route to the destination. Routing through preferred hubs
for loyalty programme reasons is not permitted where it adds cost.

## 5. Hotels

### 5.1 Nightly rate limits

| City | Per-night limit (room only, incl. taxes) |
| --- | --- |
| Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad | ₹6,000 |
| Other Tier 1 cities | ₹4,500 |
| Other cities | ₹3,000 |
| International | As per country per diem schedule (see Finance) |

### 5.2 Preferred properties

Zuqah Technologies has negotiated rates with a small number of hotel chains.
The preferred property list is in the travel portal. Use a preferred property
if one is available and convenient for the business location.

### 5.3 Duration

Book hotels only for the nights that business activity requires. Staying
additional nights for personal reasons is not reimbursed.

## 6. Changes and cancellations

### 6.1 How to change a booking

For bookings made through the company travel portal, changes can be made
through the same portal. For bookings made through the travel agent, email
travel@zuqahtechnologies.com.

### 6.2 Cancellations due to business reasons

Where travel is cancelled for business reasons (client cancels, project delays),
any cancellation fees are covered by Zuqah Technologies. Submit the cancellation
confirmation with your expense claim and note the business reason.

### 6.3 Cancellations due to personal reasons

Where travel is cancelled for personal reasons after the booking has been made,
cancellation fees are the employee's responsibility unless the personal reason
is covered by travel insurance (see section 7).

## 7. Travel insurance

Zuqah Technologies provides group travel insurance for all business travel.
Coverage includes:

- Trip cancellation (covered reasons)
- Medical expenses abroad
- Emergency evacuation
- Delayed or lost baggage

The travel insurance policy document is in the HR portal under
**Travel → Travel Insurance**. Employees should carry a copy of the insurance
details card (downloadable from the portal) when travelling internationally.

Medical emergencies abroad: call the insurance assistance number on the card
before seeking treatment where possible. Emergency treatment will be arranged
and covered.

## 8. Expense claims for travel

All travel expenses must be claimed within **30 days** of travel. Claims
submitted after **90 days** are not reimbursed. See the Expense and Travel
Policy for the full reimbursement process and limits.

## 9. Contact

**Domestic booking:** Company travel portal or travel@zuqahtechnologies.com

**International travel:** travel@zuqahtechnologies.com

**Travel insurance emergencies:** Insurance assistance number on the coverage card
in the HR portal → Travel → Travel Insurance
