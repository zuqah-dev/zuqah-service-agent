# Collaboration Tools Standard

## 1. Purpose

Which tools Zuqah Technologies uses for communication and file sharing, how to share with
people outside Zuqah Technologies, and how long things are kept.

## 2. The approved set

| Purpose | Tool | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email | Zuqah Technologies Mail | External, formal, and anything needing a record |
| Chat and meetings | Zuqah Technologies Teams | Internal day-to-day, and calls |
| Document storage | Zuqah Technologies Files | All working documents |
| Client-shared workspaces | Zuqah Technologies Files, external channel | See section 4 |
| Internal knowledge | Zuqah Technologies Wiki | Documentation, runbooks, standards |

Consumer messaging and file-sharing services must not be used for Zuqah Technologies or
client information. This includes personal email, consumer cloud storage and
personal messaging applications — the restriction is about where the data ends up,
not about which company provides the tool.

## 3. Choosing between chat and email

A frequent question, and the guidance is simple:

- **Chat** for anything conversational, time-sensitive or internal.
- **Email** for anything a client will read, anything that needs a record, and
  anything a person not currently in the conversation may later need to find.

Decisions reached in chat that matter should be summarised somewhere durable —
the wiki, the engagement record, or an email. Chat is searchable but not
discoverable by someone who does not know the conversation exists.

## 4. Sharing outside Zuqah Technologies

### 4.1 Guest access

External people can be invited to a specific Teams channel or Files folder. The
invitation is granted per resource, never to the whole workspace, and requires:

- A business reason recorded on the invitation
- An expiry date, maximum **12 months**
- Approval by the resource owner

Guests can see only what they are invited to. They cannot browse, search across
Zuqah Technologies, or see the directory.

### 4.2 Sharing links

| Link type | Available to | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Specific people | Named recipients only | The default; use this |
| Anyone in Zuqah Technologies | All employees | Internal, non-sensitive |
| Anyone with the link | Anybody who has the URL | Off by default; requires approval |

"Anyone with the link" is disabled by default and is enabled per request, with an
expiry. It is worth understanding why: such a link is a credential, and a
credential forwarded in an email chain is a credential outside your control.

### 4.3 Client data

Client information is governed first by the engagement's own agreement. Where the
client's terms are stricter than this standard, the client's terms apply. Where
they are silent, this standard applies.

Client data must remain in Zuqah Technologies Files or in the client's own systems. It must
not be copied to personal storage, to a local folder synced to a personal
account, or to a device that is not Zuqah Technologies-managed.

## 5. Meeting recording

Meetings may be recorded with the consent of participants. Consent must be
explicit — announced at the start and given a chance to object — and, in some
jurisdictions, is a legal requirement rather than a courtesy.

Recordings are retained for **90 days** and then deleted automatically. A
recording that needs to be kept longer should be saved to Zuqah Technologies Files with a
stated reason.

Client meetings may be recorded only where the engagement permits it. When in
doubt, ask before pressing record rather than after.

## 6. Retention

| Content | Retained |
| --- | --- |
| Email | 7 years |
| Teams chat | 3 years |
| Teams channel messages | 7 years |
| Meeting recordings | 90 days |
| Files, active | Indefinitely |
| Files, deleted | 93 days in the recycle bin, then permanently |

Retention is applied automatically. Employees must not attempt to shorten it by
deleting content that is subject to a legal hold; where a hold applies, People
Operations or Legal will say so explicitly.

## 7. Naming and structure

Files belong in the engagement or practice folder, not in a personal folder.
Content in a personal folder is invisible to colleagues and is deleted when the
employee leaves — which is the most common way work is lost at Zuqah Technologies.

## 8. Leaving Zuqah Technologies

On departure, mailbox and files are retained per section 6 and transferred to the
line manager. Personal content should be removed before the final working day.
See the Code of Conduct on what may be treated as personal.
