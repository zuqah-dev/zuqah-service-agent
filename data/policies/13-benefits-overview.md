# Benefits Overview

## 1. Purpose and scope

A summary of the insurance, retirement and wellbeing benefits Zuqah Technologies provides.

This document covers **benefits**. It does not cover **leave** — annual leave,
sick leave, bereavement and the other categories are set out in the Paid Time Off
and Leave Policy, which is the single source for anything to do with time away
from work.

Benefits vary by country. This document describes the United States programme;
employees elsewhere should refer to their country supplement in the HR portal.

## 2. Eligibility

| Employment type | Eligible | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permanent, full time | All benefits | First day of employment |
| Permanent, part time (20+ hours) | All benefits, pro-rated where applicable | First day of employment |
| Permanent, part time (under 20 hours) | Retirement and wellbeing only | First day of employment |
| Fixed-term, 6 months or more | All benefits | First day of employment |
| Contractor | Not eligible | — |

There is no waiting period. Coverage begins on the first day, not after 30 or
90 days.

## 3. Health cover

Three plan options. All include medical, prescription, and access to virtual care.

| Plan | Employee contribution | Annual deductible | Out-of-pocket maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Core** | $0 per month | $2,000 | $6,000 |
| **Plus** | $95 per month | $750 | $3,500 |
| **Premier** | $220 per month | $250 | $2,000 |

Contributions shown are for employee-only cover. Adding a spouse or dependants
increases the contribution; the full table is in the HR portal.

Zuqah Technologies pays the entire premium for the Core plan. Employees choosing Plus or
Premier pay the difference.

### 3.1 Dental and vision

Included with all three plans at no additional cost. There is no separate
enrolment and no separate deductible.

## 4. Life and disability

- **Life insurance** — 2× base salary, provided automatically at no cost
- **Additional life** — up to a further 4× salary, employee-paid, subject to
  medical underwriting above 2×
- **Short-term disability** — 100% of salary for the first 12 weeks
- **Long-term disability** — 60% of salary thereafter, to a maximum of $12,000
  per month

Life and short-term disability require no enrolment; they apply automatically.

## 5. Retirement

Zuqah Technologies operates a 401(k) with automatic enrolment at **4%** of salary from the
first pay period. Employees may change the rate or opt out at any time.

**Zuqah Technologies matches 100% of the first 5%** of salary contributed. An employee
contributing 5% therefore receives 10% in total.

Employer contributions vest immediately. There is no vesting schedule and no
service requirement.

## 6. Wellbeing

| Benefit | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Employee assistance programme | Included, 24/7, confidential |
| Mental health sessions | 12 per year, no cost, no referral needed |
| Fitness contribution | $60 per month |
| Annual health screening | Included |
| Wellbeing day | 1 per year, in addition to annual leave |

The mental health sessions do not require going through a manager, People
Operations or a doctor. Zuqah Technologies is not told who uses them.

## 7. Learning

- **$3,000** per year for training, conferences and certifications
- Certification examination fees paid in full, including one re-sit
- **5 days** of study leave per year — see the Paid Time Off and Leave Policy

Approval is by the practice lead and is normally granted where the subject is
relevant to current or intended work.

## 8. Other

- Referral bonus of **$4,000** for a successful hire, paid after six months
- Commuter benefit, pre-tax, up to the statutory limit
- Home office allowance for fully remote employees — see the Remote and Hybrid
  Working Policy

## 9. Enrolment and changes

New starters enrol within **30 days** of joining. Missing that window means
defaulting to the Core plan until the next open enrolment.

**Open enrolment runs each November** for the following calendar year. Outside
open enrolment, changes are permitted only after a qualifying life event, and must
be made within **60 days** of it.

## 10. Questions

Anything not covered here — plan specifics, claims, a country supplement, or a
situation this document does not describe — should go to People Operations
through the HR portal. This overview is a summary and does not replace the plan
documents, which govern in the event of any difference.
