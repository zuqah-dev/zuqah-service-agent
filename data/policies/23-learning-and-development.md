# Learning and Development Policy

## 1. Purpose

Zuqah Technologies invests in its people. This policy describes the learning
budget available to every employee, how to access it, what it covers and what
is expected in return.

## 2. Learning budget

Every permanent employee receives an annual learning budget from their first
working day.

| Level | Annual budget |
| --- | --- |
| Individual Contributor | ₹25,000 per year |
| Senior / Lead | ₹40,000 per year |
| Manager | ₹50,000 per year |

Budgets are calculated per financial year (April–March) and do not carry over
to the following year. Contract and part-time employees are eligible on a
pro-rated basis.

## 3. What the budget can be used for

### 3.1 Covered

- Online courses and platforms (Coursera, Udemy, LinkedIn Learning, Pluralsight,
  A Cloud Guru, etc.)
- Technical certifications (AWS, Azure, GCP, CKAD, PMP, etc.)
- Professional certification exam fees and preparation materials
- Books (digital or physical) directly related to the role
- Technical workshops and bootcamps
- Conference attendance (see section 4)

### 3.2 Not covered

- Degree programmes (see section 5 for special arrangements)
- Courses that are purely personal and have no connection to the current role
  or a plausible next role at Zuqah Technologies
- Equipment (laptops, monitors, peripherals) — see the Hardware Refresh Policy
- Courses already mandated and paid for by Zuqah Technologies (POSH training,
  security awareness, etc.)

If in doubt about whether something qualifies, ask People Operations before
purchasing.

## 4. Conference attendance

### 4.1 Within learning budget

A single conference may be funded from the learning budget if the registration
fee falls within the remaining budget.

### 4.2 Additional budget for conferences

Where the conference fee plus travel and accommodation would exceed the learning
budget, the employee may request additional funding from People Operations.
Approval is at the manager's discretion, subject to team budget availability.

### 4.3 What is expected

Employees who attend a conference are expected to share at least one key
takeaway with the team — a short talk, a written summary or a demo — within
two weeks of returning.

## 5. Degree programmes and long-form study

Zuqah Technologies may support employees pursuing part-time postgraduate
degrees (MBA, M.Tech, executive programmes) that are directly relevant to
their role and career at the company. Support may include:

- Partial or full funding (agreed case by case)
- Study leave of up to **5 days per year** during exam periods
- Flexible working hours during intensive modules, by agreement with the manager

Degree programme support requires approval from the employee's manager and
the CEO, and a written agreement on any repayment conditions if the employee
leaves within 24 months of receiving the benefit.

## 6. How to request learning

### 6.1 Courses and certifications (within budget)

1. Discuss the learning goal with your manager to confirm it aligns with your
   development plan
2. Log in to the HR portal → **Learning → Request Learning**
3. Fill in the course name, provider, cost and expected completion date
4. Submit — your manager will approve or decline within **3 working days**
5. Once approved, purchase the course and submit the invoice for reimbursement
   through the expense system, or request a purchase order if paying directly

### 6.2 Reimbursement

Submit the receipt through the Expense and Travel Policy process within
**30 days** of purchase. Code the expense as "Learning and Development."

### 6.3 Courses not requiring prior approval

Purchases of ₹2,000 or less per item (e.g., a single book or a low-cost
online course) may be purchased and expensed without prior approval, provided
they fall within the annual budget.

## 7. Certification exam support

### 7.1 First attempt

The cost of a certification exam (e.g., AWS Solutions Architect, Microsoft
Azure Fundamentals) is covered by the learning budget with manager approval.

### 7.2 Retakes

A single retake is covered if the first attempt was unsuccessful. A second
retake requires manager approval and a note on what additional preparation
has been done.

### 7.3 Time to prepare

Employees may use up to **2 working days** of study leave per certification
attempt, to be agreed in advance with the manager. Study leave is in addition
to the learning budget and is paid.

## 8. Internal learning

In addition to the individual budget, Zuqah Technologies provides:

- Internal knowledge-sharing sessions (Lunch & Learn, tech talks)
- Peer mentoring — employees may request to be paired with a more senior
  colleague for structured mentoring
- Manager training for all people managers, provided by the company
- Onboarding programme for new starters (see Onboarding Policy)

Participation in internal sessions does not count against the learning budget.

## 9. Learning and performance reviews

Learning goals are discussed at the beginning of each performance cycle and
reviewed at the mid-year check-in and annual review. Actively pursuing
development is a positive indicator in performance conversations.

## 10. If you leave Zuqah Technologies

### 10.1 Certifications

Certifications are yours — they go with you. Zuqah Technologies does not ask
for repayment of certification costs on resignation.

### 10.2 Degree programmes

If an employee leaves Zuqah Technologies within 24 months of receiving degree
programme funding of more than ₹50,000, a pro-rated repayment applies as
agreed in the support letter signed at the time of the award.

### 10.3 Unused budget

Unused learning budget cannot be paid out on departure.
