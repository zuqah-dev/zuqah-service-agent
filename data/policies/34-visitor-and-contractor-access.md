# Visitor and Contractor IT Access Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy covers how guests, clients and contractors are given access to
Zuqah Technologies' premises network and IT systems, and what obligations
apply to them while on-site.

## 2. Visitors (clients and guests)

### 2.1 Guest WiFi

Zuqah Technologies provides a separate guest WiFi network for visitors. Guest
WiFi provides internet access only — it cannot reach any internal company
systems, file shares or applications.

**Network name (SSID):** `Zuqah-Guest`

**How to obtain the password:** Ask the receptionist or your Zuqah Technologies
host. The password rotates weekly and is not published.

Visitors do not need to request guest WiFi access in advance; the host can
provide the current password at the time of the visit.

### 2.2 Visitor registration

All visitors to Zuqah Technologies offices must be registered before arrival.
The employee hosting the visitor must:

1. Notify Facilities at facilities@zuqahtechnologies.com at least **24 hours**
   before the visit, providing the visitor's name, company and expected arrival
   time
2. Meet the visitor at reception or arrange for them to be escorted from
   reception
3. Ensure the visitor wears a visitor badge at all times in the office
4. Accompany the visitor in all areas beyond the reception and meeting rooms,
   or ensure a colleague does so

### 2.3 What visitors may not access

Visitors may not:

- Access any company system, application or internal network (other than guest WiFi)
- Be left unattended in secure areas (server room, open-plan desk areas,
  finance or HR areas)
- Plug personal devices into company network ports

## 3. External contractors

### 3.1 Definition

An external contractor is a person who is not a Zuqah Technologies employee
but who works on a Zuqah Technologies project for an extended period (typically
more than 2 weeks) and requires access to company systems to do so.

### 3.2 Contractor IT accounts

Contractor IT accounts are created by IT on request from the hiring manager.
The request must be submitted through the IT portal under
**Access → Contractor Account Request** and must include:

- Contractor's full name and personal email address
- Start date and expected end date of the engagement
- The specific systems and applications the contractor needs access to
- Confirmation that an NDA has been signed (required before account creation)

Accounts are created within **2 working days** of a complete request.

### 3.3 Access scope

Contractor accounts are provisioned with the minimum access required for the
engagement. Access to systems beyond those listed in the original request
requires a separate approval from the hiring manager.

Contractors do not automatically receive:

- Access to HR systems or payroll data
- Access to other clients' data
- Administrative rights on any system

### 3.4 Contractor devices

Contractors who use their own device to connect to Zuqah Technologies systems:

- Must comply with the MDM and BYOD Policy for any access via mobile device
- Must use the company VPN for access to internal systems
- Must comply with the IT Acceptable Use Policy and Cybersecurity Awareness
  Policy for all work conducted on company systems

Where a contractor's engagement requires access to Restricted data or high-privilege
systems, the hiring manager may arrange a company-issued device for the duration
of the engagement.

### 3.5 Account expiry

Contractor accounts are set to expire automatically on the end date specified
in the original request. If the engagement is extended:

1. The hiring manager must submit an extension request through the IT portal
   at least **5 working days** before the account expires
2. Extensions require the same approval as the original request
3. Expired accounts are not automatically reactivated after expiry — a new
   request is needed

### 3.6 Account termination on contract end

When a contractor's engagement ends:

- The hiring manager notifies IT on the final day via the IT portal
- The account is disabled immediately and deleted within 30 days
- The contractor must return any company-issued equipment on the last day

### 3.7 Background checks

Contractors accessing Confidential or Restricted data may be required to
undergo a background check (identity verification and references) before
account creation. The hiring manager is responsible for initiating this
through People Operations.

## 4. Third-party software vendors and integrations

Where a software vendor requires access to Zuqah Technologies systems
(e.g., to support a product integration), this access is managed by IT and
is governed by the vendor contract and data processing agreement. Vendor
access requests must be routed through IT Security (security@zuqahtechnologies.com)
rather than being set up directly by the business team.

## 5. Contact

**Guest WiFi password:** Reception or your host employee

**Visitor registration:** facilities@zuqahtechnologies.com

**Contractor account requests:** IT portal → Access → Contractor Account Request

**Third-party vendor access:** security@zuqahtechnologies.com
