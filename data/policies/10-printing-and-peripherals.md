# Printing and Peripherals Guide

## 1. Purpose

How to connect to Zuqah Technologies printers, what peripherals are provided, and how to
resolve the problems that come up most often. This is a practical guide rather
than a policy; where it touches entitlement, the Laptop Refresh and Hardware
Standards governs.

## 2. Printing

### 2.1 Connecting

Zuqah Technologies uses follow-me printing. There is one print queue for the whole
organisation and a job can be collected from any Zuqah Technologies printer in any office.

1. Open **Printers** in system settings.
2. Add **Zuqah Technologies-Secure-Print**. It appears automatically on the office network
   and over the VPN.
3. Print as normal. The job is held, not printed.
4. Tap your badge at any printer to release it.

Held jobs are deleted after **24 hours**. This is deliberate: it prevents
documents sitting in an output tray overnight.

### 2.2 Printing from home

Jobs can be submitted over the VPN and collected next time you are in an office,
within the 24-hour window. Zuqah Technologies does not provide or support home printers, and
home printer costs are not reimbursable.

### 2.3 Common printing problems

**The printer does not appear.** Confirm you are on the office network or
connected to the VPN. The queue is not published to the public internet.

**The badge tap does nothing.** Badges deactivate after 90 days without an office
visit. Contact Facilities, not the Service Desk.

**The job is missing.** Check it was submitted more than 24 hours ago. Re-submit
if so.

**Printing is very slow.** Large documents with high-resolution images take time
to spool. Exporting to PDF before printing usually helps substantially.

## 3. Docking stations

One docking station per employee, requested through the IT portal without
approval.

The standard dock supports two external displays, wired network, and charging
over a single cable. It works with all three laptop models in the hardware
catalogue.

### 3.1 Common docking problems

**Displays do not wake after the laptop sleeps.** Disconnect and reconnect the
cable. If it recurs daily, the dock's firmware is likely out of date — raise a
ticket, as firmware is not updated automatically.

**Only one display works.** Check the resolution and refresh rate of both.
Two displays above 4K at 60 Hz exceed the bandwidth of a single cable. Reducing
one to 30 Hz, or to 1440p, resolves it.

**The laptop does not charge from the dock.** Confirm the dock's own power supply
is connected. The dock does not charge the laptop from bus power alone, and this
is the cause the majority of the time.

**Wired network does not work through the dock.** The dock's network adapter has
its own address, so a network requiring device registration may need it
registered. Raise a ticket with the adapter address shown in network settings.

## 4. Monitors

Up to two per employee without approval. The standard model is a 27-inch
1440p display. A single 34-inch ultrawide may be requested instead of two
displays, not in addition.

Monitors are replaced on failure, not on a refresh cycle.

## 5. Headsets, keyboards and mice

Provided on request without approval. The standard headset is certified for
Zuqah Technologies Teams, which matters — uncertified devices frequently cause the echo and
one-way-audio problems reported to the Service Desk.

Replacements for items under two years old need manager approval, except where
the item has failed.

## 6. Ergonomic and accessibility equipment

Equipment needed for an accessibility or occupational health reason is **outside**
the limits in this guide and outside the approval process. Requests go to People
Operations rather than to IT, are not subject to the standard catalogue, and do
not require a business justification to a manager.

## 7. Personal peripherals

Personal keyboards, mice, headsets and monitors may be connected to a Zuqah Technologies
laptop. Personal storage devices — USB drives and external disks — may not; the
port policy blocks them, and this cannot be exempted.

To move a file to or from an unmanaged device, use Zuqah Technologies file sharing, which is
covered in the Collaboration Tools Standard.
